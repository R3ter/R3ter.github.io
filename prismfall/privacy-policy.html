Privacy Policy – Prismfall: Falling Block Game

Developer: R3ter · App: Prismfall: Falling Block Game (Android package com.r3ter.prismfall)
Effective date: November 21, 2026
Updated: November 24, 2026 (iPhone version)

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Prismfall: Falling Block Game (“the game”). The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect, store or sell any of your personal information. Anonymous game statistics are collected with Google Analytics for Firebase, as described in section 3.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your best scores, coins, bombs and lasers, settings, which how-to-play screens you have seen, how many games you have played and your purchases are saved only in the game’s private storage on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: a banner on the menu and the game-over screen, full-screen ads between some games, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to continue a game, to double the coins a game earned or for free coins in the shop. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google’s consent form, and you can change your choice at any time with the privacy button (the shield icon) on the main menu. To show, personalise (if you agreed) and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID, and device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • how ads are shown and tapped, and diagnostic information used to keep ads working and to detect fraud.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device’s settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and Remote Config

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service, to count how the game is played: when games start and end (with the game number, mode, score, level reached and seconds played), continues, the how-to-play screen, bombs and lasers used and bought with coins, which offers and ads are shown and watched, when the purchase buttons are tapped and purchases complete, when the “No internet” screen appears, when a rating is asked for, and on which screen the game was left. Firebase also records app opens, how long and how often the game is used, in-app purchases, app and device information (such as device model, operating system version and language, and your approximate country, estimated from your IP address), an app instance identifier and your device’s advertising ID. These statistics are not linked to your name or any account, and they are used only to improve the game and how it shows ads and offers.

The game also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive a few settings (for example, how often a full-screen ad is shown) without an app update; this uses a Firebase installation identifier and does not collect anything else.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can buy optional items: removing the ads, or a starter pack (no ads plus coins, bombs and lasers). Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details; the game only learns which item was bought, so it can deliver it, and restores your purchases from Google Play when you reinstall or move to a new phone.

5. Ratings (Google Play In-App Review)

Now and then, after a new best score, the game may ask Google Play to show its rating card. The card is shown and handled by Google Play; the game never learns whether or how you rated it.

6. Internet connection

The game needs the internet to load ads, so it reads your device’s own connection status (whether Android reports that the network has working internet access). The game itself does not send anything for this check.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party receiving data from the game is Google (AdMob, Firebase and Google Play), as described above, under its own privacy policy. Data sent by the Google SDKs is transmitted using encrypted connections.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Game statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase are kept for a limited time (at most 14 months). Information processed by Google is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

iPhone version (App Store)

This policy also covers the game's iPhone version from Apple's App Store. On iPhone:

  • Advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob as described above. Before AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads, the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not used and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are also asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change that choice from the game's privacy button.
  • Analytics. The iPhone version does not send game analytics to Google Firebase. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Purchases. The iPhone version has no in-app purchases.
  • Internet check. The game checks that it is online by contacting Google's connectivity check (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com). No personal data is sent with it.
