Privacy Policy – Ricochet Agent

Developer: R3ter · App: Ricochet Agent (Android package com.r3ter.ricochetagent)
Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Ricochet Agent (“the game”). The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect, store or sell any of your personal information.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (stars earned, levels unlocked or skipped) and your settings (sound on/off) are saved only in the game’s private storage on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads (a banner, full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to skip a level or get an extra bullet). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, ads shown and taps), and diagnostic information used to prevent fraud and keep ads working.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, and — only where you have allowed it — to show ads more relevant to you. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (and other places where the law requires it), the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button in the game’s pause menu.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Play without ads tracking: if you decline personalised ads, the game still works; you may see non-personalised ads instead.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party receiving data from the game is Google, as described above, under its own privacy policy. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using encrypted connections.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
