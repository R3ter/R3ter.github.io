Privacy Policy – Park It!

Developer: R3ter · App: Park It! (Android package com.r3ter.parkit)
Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Park It! (“the game”). The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect, store or sell any of your personal information.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (medals earned, levels unlocked or skipped) and your settings (steering and pedal controls, camera options) are saved only in the game’s private storage on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: full-screen ads between some levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to continue after a crash or to skip a level. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC.

Every ad request from the game is marked as child-directed and non-personalised, and limited to content suitable for all ages (rated G). This means the ads you see are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps, and your advertising ID is not used to personalise ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited information, such as:

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • how ads are shown and tapped, and diagnostic information used to keep ads working and to detect fraud.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details; the game only learns whether the “Remove ads” purchase was made, so it can stop showing ads, and restores it from Google Play when you reinstall.

4. Internet connection

The game checks whether your device is online (by contacting a Google connectivity-check address) so it can load ads and verify purchases. No personal information is sent with this check.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because every ad request is child-directed and non-personalised, no interest-based advertising is shown to anyone, including children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party receiving data from the game is Google (AdMob and Google Play), as described above, under its own privacy policy. Data sent by the Google SDKs is transmitted using encrypted connections.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
