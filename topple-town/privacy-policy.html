Privacy Policy – Topple Town

Developer: R3ter · App: Topple Town (Android package com.r3ter.toppletown)
Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Topple Town (“the game”), a demolition puzzle game. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, it never asks you to type in anything about yourself, and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. Topple Town is not directed at children; it targets players aged 13 and over.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (the stars earned on each level, how many times you started each level, the furthest level reached and the level sets you completed), the tutorial steps you have seen, your settings (music, sound and the effects quality) and a few counters the game needs to space out its ads are saved only in the game's private storage on your device. If you bought ad removal, the game also keeps a note of it there (Google Play stays the record, see section 4). This data is not sent to the developer, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Game analytics (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and improve it (for example which levels are too hard, and where players stop playing), the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config, provided by Google LLC. They receive events such as:

  • Progress: starting, finishing, failing, skipping or leaving a level, with the level, the stars earned, how much of the building landed in the target zone, how many charges were used, the time it took, why a level failed, how many times it was tried and failed in a row, and the furthest level reached;
  • Where you leave: the screen or level you were on when you switched away from the app or closed it;
  • Ads: when an ad offer, an ad break or a rewarded video was shown, and whether a rewarded video was watched to the end;
  • Purchases: when the ad-removal offer or purchase button was shown or tapped, and whether a purchase completed or failed (and why, for example cancelled);
  • Performance: when the game lowered its effects quality because the frame rate was low (with the frame rate it measured), and whether the “no internet” screen was shown;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country and app version, and an app-instance identifier created by Firebase.

Remote Config only downloads game settings (such as how often ads appear); it does not identify you personally. Where the consent form described in section 3 applies, Firebase's advertising features follow the choice you make there. Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: a banner on the level map (never while you play a level), a full-screen ad after some levels, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for help (a hint for the level, an extra charge, or skipping a level you are stuck on). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC, and are limited to content suitable for teens.

Before any ad is requested, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check what consent, if any, is required where you are and, where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, or under relevant US state privacy laws), shows you a consent form. Ads are personalised only where you consent; otherwise they are non-personalised. Because Topple Town is not a Families-policy app (it targets players 13 and over, not children), the game may use your device's advertising ID for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, and — where you have consented — for ad personalisation. Where the law gives you the right to change your choice later, the game's menu has a Privacy option that opens the consent form again. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as device and app information, your IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), your advertising ID where applicable, and how ads are shown and tapped. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can remove the banner and the ad breaks with a one-time purchase (or a sale price offered instead); rewarded videos stay available because you choose to watch them. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details; the game only learns which product was bought, so it can deliver it, and restores the ad removal from Google Play on every launch and on a new phone (a reinstall gets it back; a refund removes it).

5. Internet connection

The game is funded by ads, so it needs an internet connection to play: it checks whether your device is online using Android's network status (or, if that is unavailable, a request to Google's connectivity check at connectivitycheck.gstatic.com), and shows a “no internet” screen until you are back online. No personal information is sent with this check. A player who has purchased ad removal (remove_ads or remove_ads_sale) is never blocked by this screen and can play without an internet connection.

6. Children

Topple Town is not directed at children under 13 and is not offered under Google Play's Families policy. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share your personal information, and does not sell personal data. The only third parties receiving data from the game are Google (Firebase, AdMob and Google Play), as described above, each under its own privacy policy. Data sent by the Google SDKs is transmitted using encrypted connections.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and you can ask for analytics data linked to your app instance to be deleted by writing to the address below.
