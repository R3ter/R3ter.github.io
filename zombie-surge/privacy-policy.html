Privacy Policy – Zombie Surge

Developer: R3ter · App: Zombie Surge (Android package com.r3ter.zombiesurge)
Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Zombie Surge (“the game”). The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. Zombie Surge is not directed at children; it targets players aged 13 and over.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (best score, runs played), coins, guns unlocked, Armory upgrades, daily gift days and your settings are saved only in the game's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to the developer, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Game analytics (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and improve it (for example which waves are too hard, and where players stop playing), the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config, provided by Google LLC. They receive events such as:

  • Progress: starting, ending or leaving a run, waves cleared, new best scores, your best score and the number of runs played;
  • Where you leave: the screen or wave you were on when you switched away from the app or closed it;
  • Ads: when an ad offer, a banner, an interstitial or a rewarded video was shown, and whether a rewarded video was watched to the end;
  • Purchases: when a shop offer or purchase button was shown or tapped, and when a purchase completed;
  • Game features: tutorials, daily gifts, upgrades bought, guns unlocked and power-ups picked up;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country and app version, and an app-instance identifier created by Firebase.

Remote Config only downloads game settings (such as how often ads appear); it does not identify you personally. Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: a banner on some screens, a full-screen ad after some finished runs, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a reward (a second chance, double coins, free coins, or to try or borrow a gun). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC.

Before any ad is requested, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check what consent, if any, is required where you are and, where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, or under relevant US state privacy laws), shows you a consent form. Ads are personalised only where you consent; otherwise they are non-personalised. Because Zombie Surge is not a Families-policy app (it targets players 13 and over, not children), the game may use your device's advertising ID for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, and — where you have consented — for ad personalisation. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time from the game's settings ("Privacy options").

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as device and app information, your IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), your advertising ID where applicable, and how ads are shown and tapped. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase (or a sale price offered instead), buy a starter pack, or buy coin packs. Guns and upgrades can also be earned with coins from playing. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details; the game only learns which products were bought, so it can deliver them, and restores the ad removal from Google Play on every launch and on a new phone (a reinstall gets it back).

5. Internet connection

The game is funded by ads, so it normally needs an internet connection to play: it checks whether your device is online using Android's network status, and shows a "no internet" screen until you are back online. No personal information is sent with this check. A player who has purchased ad removal (remove_ads, remove_ads_sale or starter_pack) is exempt from this check and can play without an internet connection.

6. Children

Zombie Surge is not directed at children under 13 and is not offered under Google Play's Families policy. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share your personal information, and does not sell personal data. The only third parties receiving data from the game are Google (Firebase, AdMob and Google Play), as described above, each under its own privacy policy. Data sent by the Google SDKs is transmitted using encrypted connections.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. You can ask for analytics data linked to your app instance to be deleted by writing to the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
