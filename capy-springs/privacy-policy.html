Privacy Policy – Capy Springs

Developer: R3ter · App: Capy Springs (Android package com.r3ter.capysprings)
Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Capy Springs (“the game”). The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (levels solved, daily puzzle streak), coins, hints, outfits and pool themes, and your settings are saved only in the game’s private storage on your device. This data is not sent to the developer, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Game analytics (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and improve it (for example which levels are too hard, and where players stop playing), the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config, provided by Google LLC. They receive:

  • game events, such as starting, solving or leaving a level, using a hint, watching an optional video, opening the shop or a purchase being completed;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country and app version, and an app-instance identifier created by Firebase.

The game does not collect your advertising ID, and analytics data is not used to personalise ads. Remote Config only downloads game settings (such as how often ads appear). Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: a banner while solving puzzles, a full-screen ad after some solved levels, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a reward (an extra heart, a hint, double coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC.

Every ad request from the game is non-personalised and limited to content suitable for all ages (rated G). The ads you see are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps, and your advertising ID is not used. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited information, such as device and app information, your IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location) and how ads are shown and tapped.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase and buy coin packs or a starter pack. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details; the game only learns which products were bought, so it can deliver them, and restores the ad removal from Google Play when you reinstall.

5. Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to play, because it is funded by ads. It checks whether your device is online using Android’s network status; no personal information is sent with this check.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ads are non-personalised and rated for all ages for every player. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party receiving data from the game is Google (Firebase, AdMob and Google Play), as described above, under its own privacy policy. Data sent by the Google SDKs is transmitted using encrypted connections.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. You can ask for analytics data linked to your app instance to be deleted by writing to the address below.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
