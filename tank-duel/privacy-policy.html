Privacy Policy – Tank Duel

Developer: R3ter · App: Tank Duel (Android package com.r3ter.tankduel)
Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Tank Duel (“the game”). The game has no accounts and no sign-in. The developer does not sell your personal information.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (levels won and their stars, coins, tanks and upgrades, the daily gift streak) and your settings (music, sound, vibration) are saved only in the game’s private storage on your device, together with whether you bought “No ads” or the starter pack. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which levels are too hard, where players stop and how the ads and the shop are used, the game sends pseudonymous usage events (tied to an app instance and advertising ID, not to your name) to Google Analytics for Firebase, part of Google LLC: levels started, won, lost or left (with the level, the attempt number, the time taken and the tank used), the screen you were on when you left the game, ads offered and watched, shop offers, taps on buy and completed purchases, the daily gift, coins spent on tanks, upgrades and continues, and when the game could not reach the internet. Firebase also records app opens, session length, the device model, operating system, language and country (estimated from the IP address), and an app instance identifier and your device’s advertising ID. This information is not linked to your name or account. The game also downloads settings (such as how often ads show) through Firebase Remote Config.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between some levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a free continue or double coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. Ads may be personalised: Google may use your device’s advertising ID and information about how you use apps to show ads that fit your interests. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with PRIVACY in the game’s settings. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes:

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • how ads are shown and tapped, and diagnostic information used to keep ads working and to detect fraud.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can remove the banner and the full-screen ads with a one-time purchase (“No ads”, sometimes at a sale price), or buy the starter pack (no ads, 2000 coins and the Heavy tank). Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details; the game only learns which of its products you own, so it can turn the ads off, and restores them from Google Play when you reinstall.

5. Internet connection

The game needs the internet to be played, because it is paid for by ads. It asks Android whether your device currently has working internet access; nothing is sent for this check. If you bought “No ads” or the starter pack, you can play offline.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, please contact us and we will help remove any information we can.

7. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell or rent personal information. The only third party receiving data from the game is Google (Firebase, AdMob and Google Play), as described above, under its own privacy policy. AdMob may share data with its advertising partners to show and measure ads. All of this data is sent over encrypted connections.

8. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Google Analytics for Firebase keeps event data for up to 14 months. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings. To ask for analytics data linked to your device to be deleted, contact us with the request.
