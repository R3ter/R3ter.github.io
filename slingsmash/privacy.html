Privacy Policy – Sling Smash

Developer: R3ter · App: Sling Smash (Android package com.r3ter.slingsmash)
Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Sling Smash (“the game”). The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not sell any of your personal information. The game sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase (section 3) so the developer can see how the game is played and improve it.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress (stars earned, levels unlocked or skipped, coins, power-up stock, daily gift streak), your settings (sound/music on or off) and whether you have bought Remove Ads are saved only in the game’s private storage on your device. This saved data is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads: a banner on the title and level-map screens, full-screen (interstitial) ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a continue, a power-up, double coins, or to skip a level. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city);
  • how you interact with ads (for example, ads shown and taps), and diagnostic information used to prevent fraud and keep ads working.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, and — only where you have allowed it — to show ads more relevant to you. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and Remote Config

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service, to count how the game is played: which levels are started, won, failed, skipped or left (with the level number, attempt number, stars, seconds played and shots left), which offers and ads are shown and watched, when purchase buttons are tapped and purchases complete, daily-gift use, continues and power-ups used, when the “No internet” screen appears, and on which screen the game was left. Firebase also records app opens, how long and how often the game is used, app and device information (such as device model, operating system version and language, and your approximate country, estimated from your IP address) and an app instance identifier; together with Google AdMob it may use your device’s advertising ID. These statistics are not linked to your name or any account, and they are used only to improve the game and how it shows ads and offers.

The game also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive a few settings (for example, how often a full-screen ad is shown, or whether an internet connection is required) without an app update; this uses a Firebase installation identifier and does not collect anything else.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase. Google keeps user-level statistics for a limited time set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months), and keeps aggregated reports for longer.

4. In-app purchases

You can buy Remove Ads (or, during a sale, Remove Ads Sale) once to turn off the banner and the full-screen ads, or the Starter Pack (Remove Ads plus a bonus of coins and power-ups). All purchases are handled entirely by Google Play: the developer never sees your name, email address or payment details. Google Play tells the game only that the purchase was made (with an order number and a purchase token), so the game can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall the game or move to a new device. The game keeps only a note on your device that the purchase is owned. Google’s handling of payments is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Internet connection requirement

Sling Smash requires an internet connection to play — it earns from ads, which need the internet — unless you have bought Remove Ads. If you lose your connection, a “No internet” panel appears until you’re back online; a level in progress pauses rather than losing your place. To check that you are really online, the game sends a simple request to Google’s connectivity check service (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com). No personal information is included; like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to Google.

6. Your choices and consent

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (and other places where the law requires it), the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game’s settings. The same choice decides whether Google Analytics for Firebase may store identifiers on your device: if you do not consent, it only sends statistics without them.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • Play without ads tracking: if you decline personalised ads, the game still works; you may see non-personalised ads instead.

7. Children

Sling Smash is rated for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third parties receiving data from the game are Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase (including Firebase Remote Config), Google Play Billing and the connectivity check, as described above, each under its own privacy policy. Data is sent using encrypted connections.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its app data. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google’s policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings, or request its deletion by contacting us (section 11).
